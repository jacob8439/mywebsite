About Me

My name is Yakub Yakubov, and I am the owner of Yakubov's Auto Detailing. I am a hardworking individual who recently moved to the United States, and I am proud to serve the people of Michigan with dedication, honesty, and care. Every day, I strive to build a business that reflects who I am — committed, motivated, and always willing to go the extra mile to deliver exceptional results.

Ever since I was young, I have had a deep love for cars. That passion has only grown stronger as I’ve learned more about what makes each vehicle unique. To me, detailing is more than just cleaning — it’s about restoring life, beauty, and pride back into every car I touch. Whether it’s reviving a worn interior, correcting paint, or giving a vehicle a showroom shine, my goal is to make every customer feel excited to drive their car again.

Michigan is now my home, and I am committed to giving my best to the community that welcomed me. I treat every vehicle like it’s my own, and I want every client to feel valued and cared for. At Pristine Auto Detailing, quality is my first priority — bringing cars back to life is not just my job, it’s something I truly enjoy.

Thank you for trusting me with your vehicle. I look forward to working with you and helping keep your car looking its absolute best.
