About the Owner

Meet Yakub Yakubov

My name is Yakub Yakubov, and I am the owner and operator of Yakubov's Auto Detailing. I am a hardworking individual who recently moved to the United States, and I’m grateful for the opportunity to serve the people of Michigan. My mission is simple: provide honest, high-quality detailing services that customers can rely on.

I come from a background that values dedication, craftsmanship, and consistency. Those values guide my approach to detailing — every vehicle is treated with care, patience, and respect. Whether it’s a daily driver or a cherished project car, I treat every vehicle as if it were my own.

A Lifelong Passion for Cars

Cars have always been a huge part of my life. What started as a simple fascination turned into a deep passion for restoring, improving, and protecting vehicles of all kinds. Detailing, to me, is more than just a service — it’s an art and a responsibility.

My first priority is to bring life back into every vehicle I work on. From correcting paint to reviving interiors and enhancing long-term protection, I focus on results that customers can see and feel. I take pride in transforming vehicles and helping owners fall in love with their cars all over again.

Serving the People of Michigan

Michigan has become my new home, and I am committed to giving back through reliable, detail-oriented work. I aim to build strong relationships with my clients through professionalism, transparency, and exceptional service.

When you choose Yakubov's Auto Detailing, you’re supporting a local small business driven by real passion, real effort, and real care for the community.
